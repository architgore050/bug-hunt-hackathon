# Terms of Use (Fall Back platform)

These terms govern use of the Bug Hunt CTF platform for the Fall Back event
of the Software Development Club (SDC), VIT Bhopal University, proposed for
6 December 2026. They are v1 draft language; formal legal review is a
pre-publication checklist item before these terms are shown as final at
/terms. Where these terms overlap with the competition rules published
through the platform, the rules document is authoritative for competition
matters and these terms govern platform use.

## 1. Purpose limitation

The platform exists to run this competition: delivering the challenge
assignment, accepting structured bug reports and flag submissions, scoring
them, and publishing results. It is not a general-purpose service, not a
file host, and not a communication product. Organizers may limit or withdraw
any feature that is not needed to run the event.

## 2. Accounts and eligibility

1. Access requires an account activated through an organizer-issued invite.
   Invites are single use and expire; the account remains tied to the email
   the organizers registered.
2. Accounts are personal: one person per account, no sharing, no transfer.
   You are responsible for all activity performed with your credentials.
   Password reset revokes all sessions for the account.
3. The organizers may suspend or disqualify accounts that break these terms
   or the competition rules. A disqualified account is refused at sign-in
   with a contact-the-organizers message.

## 3. Acceptable use

The conduct rules of the competition apply here in full. In summary:

1. You attack only the provided challenge application, on your own machine,
   inside the event window.
2. You do not attack the platform, the venue network, other teams, or any
   external system. Probing, scanning, or attempting to read ground truth,
   other teams' work, or staff-only data through the API is prohibited.
3. Automated scraping of challenge content, probing the evaluation pipeline
   with template reports, and attempts to bypass caps, cooldowns, rate
   limits, or the round clock are prohibited.
4. AI tools are prohibited for the main scored task per the event AI policy;
   the venue network enforces this and your rules acceptance covers it.
5. Good-faith reports about the platform itself are welcome under the safe
   harbor in the rules and do not breach these terms when delivered privately
   without exploitation beyond the minimum needed to demonstrate the issue.

## 4. Data processing and consent

Platform data processing is described in the privacy notice
(docs/PRIVACY_NOTICE.md and the /privacy page). By activating your account
you accept the rules version current at activation, which includes the data
processing described there; acceptance is recorded per rules version, and a
new version requires a new acceptance before submissions continue. Consent
is therefore versioned and auditable, not implied.

## 5. Intellectual property

1. Your reports. You own the text of the bug reports and evidence you write.
   You grant the club a limited license to use, store, and display them for
   running, scoring, and archiving the event, and in aggregated form for
   teaching and next-edition planning. You are credited if a finding is
   featured in the retrospective.
2. The platform and event materials. The club owns the platform code, the
   event configuration, the rules and documents, and the challenge materials.
3. The challenge app. The challenge application is licensed to you for the
   duration of the event only, for use inside your team and on your own
   machine. You may not redistribute it, publish it, or reuse its contents
   after the event without the club's written permission.
4. Third-party components keep their own licenses (the challenge app's
   dependencies and the platform's open-source dependencies are listed in
   their respective manifests).

## 6. Availability and no warranty

The platform is provided for the Fall Back event as is, on a single-VM
free-tier deployment (D0/D1 profile) with no service level agreement. The
organizers keep the competition fair and time-accurate by design
(server-owned clocks, transactional receipts, audited controls), but they do
not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free operation. Verified platform
outages are handled through the announced time-adjustment process with
recorded reason codes, not through ad hoc extensions. To the extent permitted
by law, the club is not liable for indirect or consequential damages arising
from use of the platform.

## 7. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the club, its coordinators,
and its volunteers are not liable for loss of data on your machine, lost
contest time not covered by a time adjustment, indirect or consequential
damages, or damages arising from use of the platform outside the event's
purpose. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by
law.

## 8. Termination and disqualification effects

1. You can end your use of the platform at any time by signing out and
   contacting the organizers; event records are retained per the privacy
   notice.
2. The organizers can suspend or disqualify an account or team for breaches
   listed in the competition rules (scope violations, sharing, AI use on the
   main task, account sharing, evaluation probing). Effects: platform access
   ends (sign-in refused), affected submissions are marked, and any score
   correction is applied through the score ledger so the audit trail stays
   intact and reconstructable.
3. Decisions on disqualification are recorded with reasons; disputes over
   results follow the rules' dispute window, and dispute resolutions are
   final.
